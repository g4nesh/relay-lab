No communication is trained separately. Zero and shuffled payloads intervene on the same trained checkpoint. They answer different questions.

Structured text carries exact local measurements as JSON. Embeddings carry a learned 64D message from the peer’s model features. Hybrid adds exact safety fields. This is a comparison of these complete interfaces, not a claim that their information content is identical.
