What we can and cannot measure.

Waiting is the sum of the two arms’ recorded wait ticks divided by twice the episode ticks. It is not worker utilization or human labor saved. Wall time includes the recorded runtime, not a factory throughput estimate. Alignment error is a simulator garment measure.

Labor, financial savings, physical battery use, customer adoption, and real-world reliability are not measured here. Admin permissions and live company dashboards are future product work; switching this view does not grant access rights.
